What the Data Cannot Test (Honest Limitations):-
While this dataset is great for looking at recorded prices over time, Ms. Noor's inbox highlights several things this data cannot answer:

Consumer Behavior & Sentiment: The data tells us what items cost, but it cannot tell us if people are actually changing how much they buy, feeling stressed, or skipping meals ("people just spend differently").

Quality & Brand Differences: The items are generalized categories (e.g., "Staple A"), so we can't tell if stores are selling lower-quality goods for the same price (shrinkflation/quality dilution).

Informal Markets: This dataset only covers specific tracked locations (City 1, City 2), missing local neighborhood corner stores or informal weekly markets where many people actually shop.
