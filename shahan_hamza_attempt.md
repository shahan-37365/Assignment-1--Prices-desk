Part 1: From Loud Claims to Testable and answerble Questions
Here are the two  claims I've read, turned into research questions with the necessary variables:
Claim 1: "Every basic food item has gotten expensive."
Answerble Question: Did the average price of staple food items increase significantly between January and March 2026?

Required Variable: price (segmented by item and date).

Claim 2: "Prices are fine, people just spend differently across different locations."
Testable Question: Is the percentage change in weekly prices identical across different cities for the same food items?

Required Variables: city, price, item, and date.
